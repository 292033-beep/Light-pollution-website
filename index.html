01 — WHAT IS LIGHT POLLUTION?

Light pollution is the excessive or poorly directed use of artificial light at night.

Artificial light can make faint stars and other celestial objects increasingly difficult to see.

03 — MY DOCUMENTATIONS

The effects of artificial light connects to multiple different factors such as environmental effects, human health, scientific reaserches, and cultures.
